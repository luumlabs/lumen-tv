The remote's power, input and HDMI keys on every TV, more reliable input switching, more memory for
your video apps, and Now Playing in the Control Center.

## Remote

- **The power key turns the TV off from the home screen.** Some firmwares don't act on the remote's
  power key themselves but leave it to their own launcher, so with Lumen in its place it did
  nothing. Lumen now puts the TV to sleep itself (or powers it down, with *Full power off* set),
  from the home screen, Settings, the photo frame and the screensaver. If the TV can't be reached
  that way, a menu offers to power off or restart instead, or to allow local debugging, which this
  needs.
- **Settings, input, HDMI 1–4, TV, guide and channel keys** work on the home screen too, on the
  same firmwares: settings opens the TV's own settings, input lists the inputs (or opens the TV's
  own picker on TCL and Coocaa), the HDMI keys switch straight to that port, and the TV, guide and
  channel keys open live TV.
- **Remote keys no longer detour through the home guard while Lumen is the default home.** On
  Android 11 and earlier, with the guard on, every key in every app went through it, which can
  delay keys and, in some apps, make a press read as held and move twice. The guard only needs them
  when another launcher is the default.

## Inputs

- **More reliable HDMI switching.** With a second app for TV channels installed, Android asked which
  one to use every time; Lumen now picks the TV's own. Where no app takes the input at all, Lumen
  switches as the remote's HDMI key would, through local debugging.
- **A message when switching fails**, instead of nothing, from the Control Center and from inputs
  pinned to the home screen.

## Performance

- **More memory for video apps.** The home screen is kept in memory so it comes back at once, but it
  held on to the app icons and Top 10 pictures it had drawn while another app played. Those now go
  as soon as it is hidden, leaving the memory to the app in front, its player and its streaming
  engine, which matters most on TVs with 2 GB of memory or less. On those TVs the icon cache is
  also smaller.

## Control Center

- **Now Playing.** While something plays on the TV (music, a podcast, a video in another app), its
  title, artist and app appear at the top of the Control Center: press OK to play or pause, or skip
  to the next track. It reads only what the playing app publishes about itself.
