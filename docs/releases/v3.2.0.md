# Lumen TV 3.2.0

## Changes

- **The home background follows the focused app.** A diagonal wash in the app's dominant colour
  cross-fades over ~400 ms as you move through the grid, instead of a slideshow of unrelated
  photos. Every app contributes a colour, whether it has a banner or not.
- **`android:logo` is now used before falling back to the icon**, after `android:banner`. Some
  apps ship a 16:9 logo instead of a banner and were being drawn from their square icon.
- **New gradient tiles**: the blurred crop of the icon is gone, replaced by a liquid-glass
  gradient built from the app's own colour, with a specular highlight.

## Known gaps

- Focus animations still use the original tween; a spring with overshoot is next.
