The home screen now takes on the colour of the app you are on.

## Improvements

- **A background that follows focus.** As you move through your apps, the background fades into
  the focused app's colour, so the home screen feels part of the grid rather than a slideshow
  behind it.
- **Better artwork for more apps.** Apps that provide a wide logo instead of a TV banner now show
  that logo, instead of falling back to their square icon.
- **Refined gradient tiles** for apps without a banner, built from the app's own colour.
