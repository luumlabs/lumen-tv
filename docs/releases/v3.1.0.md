New tiles for apps that don't ship a TV banner.

## Improvements

- **Liquid Glass tiles.** Apps that only provide a phone icon used to sit on a blurred crop of that
  icon. They now get a glass-like gradient in the app's own colour, lighter at the top left and
  deeper at the bottom right, with a soft highlight in the corner, much closer to tvOS 26.
- Apps that ship a TV banner keep using it.

---

**Download:** `Lumen-v3.1.0.apk` (standard build). Newer releases also ship a guard build for TCL and FFalcon China firmware.
See the [installation guide](https://github.com/luumlabs/lumen-tv#installation).
