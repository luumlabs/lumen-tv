# Lumen TV 3.1.0

## Changes

- **App tiles now use a Liquid Glass gradient instead of a blurred crop of the icon.** The colour
  comes from the app's own artwork (the most vibrant swatch Palette finds), mixed to a light tone
  at the top-left and a deep one at the bottom-right, with a specular highlight in the corner.
  Cleaner, calmer, and much closer to tvOS 26.
- Apps that ship a banner still use it; nothing changes for them.
