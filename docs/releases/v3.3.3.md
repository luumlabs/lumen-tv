Fixes the dock losing its place.

## Fixes

- **The dock stays with its apps.** After rearranging apps in *Edit Home Screen*, after an app was
  installed or removed, or when scrolling was interrupted, the glass dock could stay at the top of
  the screen while the apps returned to the bottom, leaving an empty glass panel above them. The
  dock now always follows the first row of apps.

No other visible change: the dock sits behind the first row and slides up with it when you expand
the app grid.
