A reliability release for slower TVs.

## Fixes

- **No more crash on startup on slow TVs.** On a cold start, the system could close the home screen
  because a background service took too long to report itself, leaving a half-drawn screen and
  sending the Home key back to the TV's own launcher. The service now starts in time on every TV.
- **The dock no longer draws half-finished** when the app list reloads, for example after an app is
  installed or updated.

Both issues were reproduced and confirmed fixed on a TCL G05 running Android 11. The look of the
home screen is unchanged.
