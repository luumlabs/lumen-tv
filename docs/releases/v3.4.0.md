# Lumen TV 3.4.0

A redesign after tvOS 26 and 27, and two widgets.

## Widgets

- **Weather**, above the dock, laid out like Apple's medium Weather widget: the city, the
  temperature, the condition, the day's high and low and the next six hours, on glass tinted with
  the colour of the sky. Press OK for a six-day forecast. Weather by Open-Meteo; the TV is placed
  by its internet address, or by a city set with `--es weather_city "Madrid"`.
- **Top 10**: this week's most popular movies and shows in your country, and the best reviewed of
  them, from JustWatch. The widget turns from chart to chart; press OK to browse them full screen:
  the title's backdrop, its rank, details, scores on IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, TMDB and JustWatch, the
  cast and where it streams. Left and right move along a chart, up and down switch charts, OK opens
  the streaming app when it is installed.
- Both can be turned off in Settings → Widgets.

## Look and feel

- Every focus change, sheet and panel moves on a real spring instead of an overshoot curve.
- Tiles are layered glass: icon-only apps float their icon above a gradient, and the focus
  parallax moves the icon twice as far as the background. A lit rim catches the light, a focused
  tile drifts gently while you rest (stopping after 45 seconds without a key), and OK presses it in.
- The background glows in the focused app's three colours, and the dock's glass refracts that
  colour instead of the bare wallpaper. Settings → Appearance turns it off.
- The dock's Liquid Glass gained dispersion at the rim, a bevel glow, a sheen and a shaded lower
  edge.
- Folders open over the frosted screen as a grid of tiles; folder menus are glass sheets.
- Alerts are frosted, with glass buttons that spring in and scroll when there are many.
- Control Center opens faster, its rows following one another in.
- Settings starts with categories, as on Apple TV; rows turn into a lifted white pane and the list
  glides to keep the focused row in the middle.

## Fixes

- Dates were shown in Chinese on the large clock and in the photo frame; the frame's clock now
  follows the 12/24-hour setting.
- Rows in Settings that start something ("Setting up…", "Downloading…") lost their progress text at
  once.
- Top Shelf counts read "48.4K" instead of "48.4×10K"; the screensaver's name is in English.
