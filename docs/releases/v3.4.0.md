A redesign modelled on tvOS 26 and 27, and two new widgets: the weather and this week's Top 10.

## New: widgets

- **Weather**, on glass above the dock, laid out like Apple's Weather widget: the city, the
  temperature, the conditions, the day's high and low and the next six hours, on a sky that changes
  with the weather and the time of day. Press OK for a six-day forecast.
- **Top 10**: this week's most popular movies and shows in your country, and the best reviewed of
  them. Press OK to browse full screen, with each title's backdrop, rank, details, scores from IMDb,
  Rotten Tomatoes, TMDB and JustWatch, the cast and where it streams. Left and right move through a
  chart, up and down switch charts, and OK opens the streaming app when it is installed.
- Both can be turned off, and set to a city, unit or country, in **Settings → Widgets**.

## New look and feel

- **Natural motion.** Every focus change, alert and panel now moves on a real spring.
- **Layered glass tiles.** Apps without a TV banner float their icon above a gradient, and the icon
  moves further than the background as the tile tilts. Every tile has a glass rim that catches the
  light, presses in when you select it, and drifts gently while you rest on it.
- **Colour that belongs to the app.** The background glows in the three main colours of the focused
  app, and the dock's glass refracts that colour rather than the bare wallpaper. You can turn this
  off in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **Richer Liquid Glass** on the dock: light dispersing at the rim, a bevel glow, a sheen and a
  shaded lower edge that gives it thickness.
- **Folders** open over the frosted screen as a grid of tiles.
- **Alerts** are frosted, with glass buttons that slide in and scroll when there are many.
- **A faster Control Center**, its controls arriving one after another.
- **A redesigned Settings app**, organised into categories like Apple TV's, with the selected row
  lifting into a white panel and the list keeping it centred.

## Fixes

- Dates on the large clock and in the photo frame were shown in Chinese. The photo frame's clock
  now also follows the 12/24-hour setting.
- Settings rows that start a task, such as *Change now* or *Free memory now*, showed their progress
  text only for an instant.
- View counts on the Top Shelf now read as "48.4K", and the screensaver appears under an English
  name in the TV's settings.
