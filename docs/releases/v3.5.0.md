Clear-glass widgets, a new Top 10 and smoother, calmer motion across the home screen.

## Widgets

- **Weather on clear glass.** The widget is now made of the same Liquid Glass as the dock, refracting
  what is behind it, with white type and monochrome symbols instead of a coloured sky. Over a bright
  wallpaper the glass darkens slightly behind the text so it stays readable. The forecast screen
  follows the same neutral style.
- **A new Top 10.** The widget now shows one title at a time over its own artwork: the rank in large
  figures beside the title, the year, genre and score, and ten dots for its place in the chart. It
  moves down the chart every few seconds and on to the next chart, pausing while you are on it.
- **Choose where to watch.** Press OK on a title, in the widget or in the full Top 10, and pick an
  app to open it in, each shown with its icon:
  - the streaming services that carry it and are installed on your TV, opened straight at the title
    when the app supports it;
  - **Nuvio** and **Stremio**, opened at the title itself (Stremio searches for it by name when the
    title's IMDb id is unknown).

  Only apps that can actually open the title on your TV are listed, and the services it streams on
  that aren't installed are named below. From the widget, *See the full Top 10* opens the charts.
- **Cleaner posters in the full Top 10.** Ranks now sit inside each poster, so they never overlap the
  poster beside them, and where a title streams joins the row of scores.

## Fixes

- **Control Center's network control** read "Connecting" or was cut off: the text didn't fit its
  capsule. It now shows *Wi-Fi* or *Ethernet* in full, with *Connected*, *Limited* (no internet) or
  *Offline*, and the matching symbol.

## Motion

- **A calmer focus.** Tiles lift on a firmer spring and lean in only slightly, settling without any
  wobble. The tile no longer drifts while you rest on it; instead a single glint of light passes
  across it once it has settled.
- **Back from an app**, the home screen settles from slightly enlarged around the tile you opened,
  the way tvOS returns to an icon.
- **Smoother navigation.** The background colour waits for focus to rest before changing, and the
  glass is re-made only once you stop moving, so sliding along a row stays fluid. The glass itself
  is now computed with a quarter of the work and memory.
